# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### What is Chef Mate?
Chef Mate is a cooking companion app that helps you save recipes from anywhere, plan your meals, build grocery lists, and cook hands-free with step-by-step Cook Mode.

### Is Chef Mate free to use?
Chef Mate is free to download and use. Premium features may be added in the future, but everything is free today.

### What platforms is Chef Mate available on?
Chef Mate is available on iOS, Android, macOS, Linux, and Windows, and stays in sync across all of them.

## Account

### How do I create an account?
Download Chef Mate from the App Store or Google Play and follow the on-screen sign-up instructions.

### How do I delete my account?
You can delete your account from any of the desktop, Android, or iOS apps:

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Select **Manage Profile**
3. Click **Delete Profile**
4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Once confirmed, your account and all associated data will be permanently removed.

## Features

### How do I save a recipe from the web?
Found a recipe while browsing in Safari, Chrome, or another app? Tap the share button and choose Chef Mate — the recipe's photo, ingredients, and steps come straight into your collection, no copying and pasting. Prefer to stay in the app? Open the built-in browser from the bottom navigation to search the web, then tap **Download** on any recipe you find.

### Can I save my favorite recipes?
Yes — tap the heart icon on any recipe to mark it as a favorite.

### Can I organize my recipes into collections?
Yes — group recipes into **recipe books**, such as weeknight dinners, holiday baking, or family favorites. Invite collaborators to a recipe book so everyone can add to and cook from the same collection.

### Can I share recipes with friends?
Yes — every recipe has a shareable link. Anyone can open it in a browser to view the ingredients and directions, with an option to save it straight into their own Chef Mate account.

### How do I add a recipe's ingredients to my grocery list?
Open the recipe and tap the cart icon in the toolbar to send its ingredients straight to a grocery list. Chef Mate combines duplicates so you never buy the same thing twice.

### Can I share a grocery list with family or roommates?
Yes — you can keep more than one grocery list and share any of them with family or roommates. Everyone's changes stay in sync as you shop.

### How do I plan my meals for the week?
Open a recipe and tap the calendar icon in the toolbar to drop it onto any day. Switch between Day, Week, and Month views from the **Meal Plan** tab to see your whole schedule.

### What is Cook Mode?
Cook Mode is a distraction-free, step-by-step view for when you're ready to cook. Tap **Start Cooking** from a recipe's toolbar — the text stays large and your screen won't dim, so you can keep both hands on the food.

### Does Chef Mate have an AI assistant?
Yes — tap the AI Chat icon to ask for recipe ideas, ingredient substitutions, or cooking tips. You can turn any answer into a new saved recipe with one tap.

## Deleting Data

### How do I delete a recipe?
Open the recipe you want to remove, tap the **menu icon** (three dots), and select **Delete Recipe**. Confirm the deletion when prompted. This will remove the recipe from your saved collection but will not affect your account or other data.

### How do I delete grocery items?
Navigate to your **Grocery List**, swipe left on the item you want to remove, and tap **Delete**. To clear your entire grocery list, tap the **menu icon** at the top of the list and select **Clear All Items**.

### How do I delete meal planning items?
Go to your **Meal Plan**, tap the meal you want to remove, and select **Remove from Plan**. To clear an entire day or week, tap the **menu icon** and select **Clear Day** or **Clear Week**.

### How do I leave or delete a recipe book?
Open the recipe book from the recipe book picker and tap the **menu icon**. Choose **Leave** if you're a collaborator, or **Delete** if you own it, and confirm when prompted. Recipes already saved to your own collection are not affected.

### Will deleting data affect my account?
No — deleting recipes, grocery items, meal plan entries, or a recipe book only removes that specific data. Your account, profile, and all other saved content remain intact.
